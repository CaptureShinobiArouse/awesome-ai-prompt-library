## 🧠 Stop Prompting in the Dark

**Awesome AI Prompt Library** is a meticulously curated, open-source repository containing over 5,000 tested prompts for Large Language Models (LLMs) and AI image generators. 

We all know that AI is only as good as the prompt it receives. But finding the right words to get exactly what you want is a skill. This library removes the guesswork. Whether you are a developer trying to generate a complex React component, a marketer writing the perfect cold email, or an artist trying to create a specific visual style, we have a prompt for you.

Organized by category, difficulty, and AI model, every prompt includes an explanation of *why* it works and variations you can try. Stop wasting hours tweaking your prompts and start getting 10x better results instantly.

> 💡 **Why use this library?** Because reinventing the wheel is a waste of time. Stand on the shoulders of giants, copy proven prompts, and get straight to the output you need.

## 🚀 Quick Start

> **Three steps. Under two minutes. No installation required.**

### 1️⃣ Browse the repository
Click the **Browse Library** button at the top to view the markdown files directly on GitHub.

### 2️⃣ Find your prompt
Use the `Ctrl+F` search function or navigate through the folders to find what you need.

### 3️⃣ Copy and paste
Copy the prompt, replace any `[VARIABLES]` with your own text, and paste it into ChatGPT or Midjourney.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

<details>
<summary><b>Are these prompts free to use?</b></summary>

Yes! The entire library is open-source under the MIT License. You can use them for personal or commercial projects without paying a dime.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Do these work on GPT-4 and Claude 3?</b></summary>

Yes. Most prompts are highly portable. However, we do provide specific variations for certain models when their quirks require different phrasing.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I contribute my own prompts?</b></summary>

Absolutely! We welcome pull requests. Please make sure to follow the template and explain *why* your prompt works.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Does it include jailbreak prompts?</b></summary>

No. We strictly curate ethical, professional, and safe prompts for productivity, coding, and creativity. We do not host bypass or jailbreak prompts.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How do I report a bug?</b></summary>

Open an issue on the GitHub repository. Include the name of the prompt and the AI model you tested it on.
</details>
